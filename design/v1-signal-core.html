About

Where I studied and worked, in order. The third ring carries one node per stop.

I started with two bachelor's degrees at once at the Lebanese University, telecommunication engineering in the top 1% alongside computer science. Then Paris: general engineering at Mines Paris with a geostatistics and applied probability specialty, a statistics and learning master at Sorbonne, and now the MASEF master at Paris Dauphine, where the courses are stochastic calculus, market microstructure, order book models and high-frequency trading.

Along the way I built trading platform tooling at Capital Fund Management, video-to-animation pose models at Kinetix, and a daily market data and backtesting platform at Debbas Markets. I ran the IEEE student branch for a year and taught a twelve-session full-stack bootcamp.
